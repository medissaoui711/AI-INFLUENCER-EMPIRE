# Decision Authority

## Reserved to the founder

The founder / CEO is the sole owner and final decision-maker for strategy, changes to approved decisions, material budget and purchases, external partnerships and contracts, access to company accounts or data, acceptance of material risks, final brand and content approval, and publication.

## Recommendations and delegated work

Research, AI outputs, and contributor proposals are recommendations. A task may be delegated only with explicit scope and access limits. Delegation does not transfer reserved authority. No person or tool may infer authority from a draft or prior phase approval.

## Escalation

Pause and escalate when an action would spend funds, access an external service, expose confidential material, create a contractual or public commitment, cause material or irreversible change, or exceed approved scope. Record the decision and rationale in the decision log where material.
