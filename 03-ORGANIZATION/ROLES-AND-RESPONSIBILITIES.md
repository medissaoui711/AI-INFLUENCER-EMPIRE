# Roles and Responsibilities

| Role or function | Current holder / status | Responsibility and authority |
|---|---|---|
| Founder / CEO | Founder; sole owner | Performs and approves all company functions; retains final authority for strategy, budget, risk, external commitments, and publication. |
| Strategy and research | Founder; AI assistance may support | Frame questions, gather evidence within approved scope, and distinguish findings from hypotheses. Founder decides. |
| Media and production | Founder; AI assistance may support | Plan and review production only when separately authorized; founder approves assets. |
| Systems development | Founder; AI assistance may support | Maintain the documented operating framework; no software build is implied. |
| Finance and analytics | Founder; AI assistance may support | Maintain approved records and evidence; founder approves all spending and financial decisions. |
| Character, quality, publishing, community, monetization, IP | Founder; AI assistance may support | Perform these responsibilities only as scoped and approved; founder retains final approval. |
| Future human contributors | Not engaged by this framework | Any future role requires a separate founder decision and defined scope, access, and accountability. |

Functional labels identify work, not staffed departments. AI systems provide assistance and have no legal or corporate authority.
