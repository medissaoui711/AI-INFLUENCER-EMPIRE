# Organization Structure

## Current state

The founder is the sole owner and CEO and currently performs all functional responsibilities. The structure below is a responsibility map, not evidence of separate departments or staff.

## Functional responsibilities

- Strategy and research
- Media and production operations
- Systems and documentation development
- Finance and analytics
- Character management
- Quality and compliance
- Publishing, distribution, audience, and community
- Monetization and product/IP library

Responsibilities may be combined by the founder according to capacity and approved scope. AI-assisted functions can support analysis and drafting but are not employees, officers, or decision-makers.

## Future capacity

Human hires or contractors may be considered only after a founder decision defines the need, budget, authority, access, and accountability. None are currently asserted as engaged.
