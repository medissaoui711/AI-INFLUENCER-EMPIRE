# Operating Lifecycle

Approved sequence: **Research → Business Design → Character Design → Production → Distribution → Conversion → Measurement → Optimization**. Progression is conditional: each stage needs adequate inputs, documented outputs, required rights and controls, and founder approval where the action has material, external, budget, or publication consequences.

| Stage | Purpose | Inputs | Expected output | Move-forward condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Research | Understand a problem, audience, market, and evidence gaps. | Research question and approved scope. | Sourced findings, uncertainties, and implications. | Evidence is adequate for a bounded business-design decision; gaps are explicit. |
| Business Design | Define a testable value proposition and operating model. | Research synthesis and constraints. | Offer hypothesis, customer, delivery outline, cost assumptions, risks. | Founder approves a test brief and its limits. |
| Character Design | Specify a character suitable for the approved proposition. | Approved strategy, audience, brief, and rights criteria. | Character profile and continuity/rights records. | Founder approves the specification and sources. |
| Production | Create a deliverable against an approved brief. | Approved profile, brief, cleared inputs, tools, and budget. | Reviewed content and production records. | Quality, rights, and claims checks pass; founder approves release candidate. |
| Distribution | Deliver approved content through chosen channels. | Release candidate, channel requirements, disclosure, and publishing approval. | Distribution record and observations. | Publication completed within scope; gather permitted measures. |
| Conversion | Test whether audience action maps to a defined value exchange. | Approved offer, path, terms, and channel. | Conversion observations and transaction records if applicable. | Results and customer obligations are recorded; no unapproved transactions. |
| Measurement | Evaluate outcomes against pre-defined questions. | Data, definitions, dates, costs, and known limits. | Evidence summary, uncertainty, and recommendation. | Founder reviews evidence and decides whether to continue, revise, or stop. |
| Optimization | Apply an approved learning to the next bounded cycle. | Reviewed measurement and decision. | Approved change, updated documents, and next test scope. | Change is recorded and approvals/controls are updated before restarting. |

No lifecycle activity beyond foundation documentation is undertaken in this phase.
