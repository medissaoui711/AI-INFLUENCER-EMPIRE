# Character System

**Mission:** Govern the definition, continuity, rights, and quality of any future AIE virtual character.

**Scope:** Character requirements, identity records, narrative constraints, provenance, permissions, and approval checkpoints.

**Inputs:** Approved strategy and audience evidence, creative brief, rights requirements, and founder direction.

**Core activities:** Define a concept; document identity and boundaries; review consistency and rights; maintain controlled versions; obtain approval before production or publication.

**Outputs:** Approved character specification, continuity references, rights records, and change history.

**Responsible role:** Founder / CEO acting as character owner and approver; AI-assisted creative support is advisory.

**Dependencies:** Strategy System, Production System, and documented source and usage rights.

**Key controls:** No character may be treated as approved until the founder approves it; track provenance; avoid misleading claims about real-person identity or consent.

**Indicators to define later:** Specification completeness, continuity review findings, and rights-record completeness. No targets or results are set.

**Out of scope now:** Selecting a niche, designing a character, generating images, cloning a voice, or publishing assets.
