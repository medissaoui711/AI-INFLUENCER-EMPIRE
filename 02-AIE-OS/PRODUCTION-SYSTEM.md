# Production System

**Mission:** Provide a repeatable, reviewable path from approved brief to controlled media deliverable.

**Scope:** Briefing, asset sourcing, production planning, review, rights checks, versioning, and handoff.

**Inputs:** Approved character and content brief, cleared assets, channel requirements, and an authorized production scope.

**Core activities:** Confirm brief and rights; plan production; create within approved tools and budget; review quality and claims; record versions and disposition.

**Outputs:** Reviewed media deliverables, production records, source references, and issue logs.

**Responsible role:** Founder / CEO as producer and final approver; future contributors are not currently engaged.

**Dependencies:** Character System, quality controls, storage, approved tools, and Marketing System requirements.

**Key controls:** Rights and provenance checks, quality review, budget approval, secure asset handling, and no publication without explicit approval.

**Indicators to define later:** Cycle time, rework, rights completeness, and production cost per deliverable. No targets or results are set.

**Out of scope now:** Media generation, voice production, video editing, tool purchase, or production automation.
