# Revenue System

**Mission:** Evaluate potential offers and conversion paths with clear evidence, financial controls, and founder approval.

**Scope:** Offer hypotheses, customer value, pricing research, conversion design, transaction controls, and revenue reporting.

**Inputs:** Validated customer problem evidence, approved offer scope, costs, rights, channel data, and founder budget decisions.

**Core activities:** Form offer hypotheses; assess delivery and costs; review terms and disclosures; seek founder approval; monitor results if a later test is authorized.

**Outputs:** Offer briefs, unit-economics assumptions, approval records, and revenue reports when applicable.

**Responsible role:** Founder / CEO retains all purchasing, pricing, budget, and contractual authority.

**Dependencies:** Business architecture, Marketing System, Finance records, rights clearance, and customer evidence.

**Key controls:** No financial promise or transaction without approval; distinguish gross receipts from costs; protect payment and customer data.

**Indicators to define later:** Conversion, revenue, contribution after defined costs, refunds, and fulfillment effort. No targets or results are set.

**Out of scope now:** Store creation, affiliate registration, sales, pricing decisions, subscriptions, or payment operations.
