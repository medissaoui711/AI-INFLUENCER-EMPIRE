# AIE-OS Overview

**AIE-OS** means **AI Influencer Empire Operating System**. It is the company's organizational and operational framework for coordinating work, decisions, and learning. In this phase it is documentation, not software or automation.

## Ownership and boundaries

The founder owns and approves the framework. AI-assisted functions may help draft, analyze, or organize work but have no independent authority. Each process requires an approved scope before execution. External actions, spending, publishing, and risk acceptance remain subject to founder approval.

## Six systems

1. **Strategy System:** direction, hypotheses, priorities, and research questions.
2. **Character System:** character concepts, identity, continuity, and rights controls.
3. **Production System:** briefs, production, review, asset handling, and quality.
4. **Marketing System:** distribution, audience interaction, and channel learning.
5. **Revenue System:** offer hypotheses, conversion paths, and financial controls.
6. **Intelligence System:** measurement, evidence quality, reporting, and learning.

## Relationship

The systems coordinate through the approved lifecycle: Research → Business Design → Character Design → Production → Distribution → Conversion → Measurement → Optimization. Measurement may inform strategy; changes return through founder review and documented approval. Inputs, outputs, dependencies, controls, indicators, and present exclusions are defined in each system document.
