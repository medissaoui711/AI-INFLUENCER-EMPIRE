# Intelligence System

**Mission:** Turn reliable observations into traceable learning that can inform founder decisions.

**Scope:** Measurement design, data definitions, collection permissions, analysis, reporting, and evidence quality.

**Inputs:** Approved questions, authorized data, platform outputs, experiment records, costs, and known limitations.

**Core activities:** Define measures before tests; record source and period; distinguish observation from interpretation; document uncertainty; present implications for decision.

**Outputs:** Measurement plans, evidence summaries, experiment reports, and recommendations.

**Responsible role:** Founder / CEO owns interpretation and decisions; AI-assisted analytics support may organize or analyze approved data.

**Dependencies:** Strategy and Marketing questions, Revenue records where applicable, privacy controls, and data access authorization.

**Key controls:** Minimize personal data; preserve source context; do not fabricate results or overstate causation; founder approves external reporting.

**Indicators to define later:** Data completeness, measurement coverage, evidence freshness, and decision traceability. No targets or results are set.

**Out of scope now:** Connecting analytics accounts, collecting audience data, building dashboards, or reporting performance.
