# Marketing System

**Mission:** Plan and evaluate responsible distribution and audience communication for approved offers and content.

**Scope:** Channel selection, publishing plans, audience interaction, campaign definition, and distribution learning.

**Inputs:** Approved strategy, cleared content, platform requirements, audience evidence, and founder-approved publishing scope.

**Core activities:** Define audience and message; select channel; prepare distribution; review claims and disclosures; publish only after approval; capture platform results.

**Outputs:** Distribution plans, approved campaign records, community guidelines, and channel observations.

**Responsible role:** Founder / CEO as publisher and final approver; AI-assisted marketing support is advisory.

**Dependencies:** Character, Production, Revenue, and Intelligence Systems; platform access and policies.

**Key controls:** No account creation or publishing without approval; truthful claims; respect privacy, platform rules, and disclosure obligations.

**Indicators to define later:** Reach, engagement quality, referral behavior, and audience feedback, interpreted with platform limitations. No targets or results are set.

**Out of scope now:** Social account creation, publishing, scheduling, paid promotion, or audience outreach.
