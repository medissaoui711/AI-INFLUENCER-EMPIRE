# Strategy System

**Mission:** Keep company direction, market questions, and experiments aligned with approved founder decisions.

**Scope:** Strategic objectives, hypotheses, research priorities, business design, and decision traceability.

**Inputs:** Founder-approved direction, evidence, customer or audience observations, risk information, and available capacity.

**Core activities:** Frame questions; distinguish facts from assumptions; compare options; propose bounded tests; record recommendations and obtain approval.

**Outputs:** Strategy updates, research briefs, prioritized hypotheses, and decision-log proposals.

**Responsible role:** Founder / CEO, supported by AI-assisted strategy and research functions; no separate staff are implied.

**Dependencies:** Market Intelligence and Intelligence System evidence; founder approval; documented scope and budget.

**Key controls:** No market or revenue claim without evidence; no material change without approval; preserve the US/English-first sequence.

**Indicators to define later:** Hypothesis status, evidence completeness, experiment decision rate, and review cadence. No targets or results are set.

**Out of scope now:** Conducting market research, selecting a niche, setting numeric goals, or spending budget.
