# Governance Policy

## Decision levels

1. **Founder decisions:** strategy, budget, external commitments, publication, risk acceptance, and material scope changes require founder approval.
2. **In-scope execution choices:** reversible editorial or organizational choices may be made by the authorized implementer when they do not change approved facts, spend money, access external accounts, or widen scope. Record material choices.
3. **Recommendations:** research, AI output, and proposed options remain recommendations until the founder approves them.

## Approval and scope

Work must have a defined phase and approved scope. Do not infer approval from a draft, recommendation, silence, or prior approval of a different phase. Stop when a material conflict, destructive operation, external commitment, or unapproved scope expansion appears.

## Change control

Record material changes with date, reason, owner, affected documents, and approval status in the change log. Update decision records when a founder-approved decision changes; retain the prior record for traceability.

## Risk escalation

Identify the evidence and impact of a risk, propose mitigations, and escalate material or uncertain risks to the founder. Only the founder may accept company risk.

## Documentation and records

Use the naming and documentation standards. Separate verified facts, approved decisions, hypotheses, unknowns, and recommendations. Protect credentials and private account data; never place secrets in company documentation or Git.
