# Company Strategy

## Approved direction

- Operate through three connected arms: AI Influencer Media, AI Influencer Agency, and AI Influencer Systems.
- Test the US / English-speaking market first; assess the Arabic-speaking market second after evaluating first-market evidence.
- Use AIE-OS as the organizational operating framework, with six systems and the documented lifecycle.
- The founder is sole owner and CEO and retains approval authority.

## Strategic intent

Use controlled research and experiments to learn whether owned virtual influencer media, client services, and reusable systems can reinforce one another. Build evidence before expanding scope, committing material budget, or making external claims.

## Working hypotheses

- Owned characters and media may create audience and IP value.
- Client services may generate learning and potential service revenue.
- Reusable workflows and templates may become useful products or improve internal efficiency.
- Lessons may transfer across the three arms, subject to customer, rights, and quality constraints.

These are hypotheses, not validated findings.

## Unvalidated assumptions

Demand, willingness to pay, viable acquisition channels, suitable production costs, platform fit, rights clearance, and repeatable audience growth are unknown. No revenue targets, audience targets, market sizing, or profitability forecasts are approved.

## Future strategic questions

Which niche and customer segment merit testing? Which capabilities should be built internally or sourced? What evidence and thresholds justify the second market? What rights, disclosure, and quality controls will each offer require? Address these in later approved work, beginning with market research.
