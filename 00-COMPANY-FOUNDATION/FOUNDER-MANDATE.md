# Founder Mandate

## Role

The founder is the sole owner and CEO and currently performs all company responsibilities. No employee, contractor, advisor, or partner is represented as engaged by this document.

## Reserved authority

The founder has final authority over:

- Company strategy, scope, and acceptance of deliverables.
- Budgets, purchases, subscriptions, and financial commitments.
- Final character, brand, content, and publication approval.
- Acceptance or treatment of material risks.
- External partnerships, client commitments, contracts, and public representations.
- Access to accounts, credentials, intellectual property, and company records.
- Changes to approved company decisions and governance documents.

## Delegation and AI assistance

Tasks may be supported by AI tools or future human contributors only within explicit scope. Recommendations, drafts, and analysis do not constitute approval or authority to transact, publish, contract, or speak for AIE. Any future delegation must define scope, access, review, and accountability before work begins.
