# Company Charter

## Purpose

Build a durable company that creates virtual influencer media, provides related services, and develops reusable systems and intellectual property.

## Vision and mission

**Vision:** Establish a trusted, adaptable operating company for virtual influencer ventures.

**Mission:** Research, design, produce, distribute, and measure virtual influencer businesses through a connected media, agency, and systems model.

These statements express direction; they are not claims of current market position or commercial success.

## Scope

AI Influencer Empire (AIE) encompasses three connected business arms: AI Influencer Media, AI Influencer Agency, and AI Influencer Systems. Their purposes and validation needs are documented in the business architecture.

## Ownership and authority

The founder is the sole owner and CEO. The founder approves strategy, budgets, external commitments, brand and content publication, accepted risks, and changes to this charter. AI-assisted work is advisory and does not hold corporate or legal authority.

## Market sequence

The first test market is the United States / English-speaking audience. The Arabic-speaking market is a second test market, considered after evidence from the first market is reviewed. Neither market is represented as validated.

## Principles

- Make claims in proportion to evidence.
- Keep recommendations distinct from approved decisions.
- Protect people, intellectual property, accounts, and confidential information.
- Prefer a manageable solo-founder operating model.
- Document consequential decisions and changes.
- Test in bounded steps and learn from measured results.

## Boundaries

AIE does not claim incorporation, registrations, customers, revenue, staff, partnerships, audience, or validated demand based on this charter. It does not promise commercial outcomes or imply that planned systems are software products already operating.
