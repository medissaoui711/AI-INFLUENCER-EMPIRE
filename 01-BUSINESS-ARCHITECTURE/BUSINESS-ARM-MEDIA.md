# Business Arm — AI Influencer Media

## Purpose

Develop and operate AIE-owned virtual influencer characters and media properties, subject to rights, platform, and quality controls.

## Audience and value

The intended audience is to be selected after market research. Potential value includes distinctive entertainment, information, or storytelling; no niche or audience need has yet been validated.

## Potential revenue hypotheses

Advertising or sponsorship, licensing, affiliate activity, and owned products may be investigated later. These are hypotheses, not offers or current revenue.

## Capabilities and dependencies

Character strategy, editorial planning, production, distribution, community care, measurement, rights tracking, and disclosure review. Dependencies include suitable tools, platform access, rights clearance, and audience response.

## Validation and risks

Validate audience problem or interest, content quality, repeatable production cost, distribution fit, and monetization evidence. Risks include platform dependency, rights disputes, synthetic-media disclosure expectations, reputational harm, and unbounded production costs.

## Current boundary

No character, account, content, or media asset is created by this foundation phase.
