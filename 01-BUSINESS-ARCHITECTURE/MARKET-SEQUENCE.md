# Market Sequence

## Approved order

1. **First test market:** United States / English-speaking audience.
2. **Second test market:** Arabic-speaking audience, considered only after evaluating the first market and identifying an approach supported by evidence.

This order is an approved strategic decision. It does not establish market demand, customer fit, or commercial viability in either market.

## Why sequence

A bounded first test can focus research, language, audience definition, and learning. Expanding to a second language and market before reviewing evidence may multiply localization and operational complexity. This is the rationale for the approved sequence, not a claim that the first market is easier or more attractive.

## Evidence before considering market two

Before a founder decision to proceed, collect and review evidence relevant to the selected offer: audience or customer problem, observed response to tests, channel fit, repeatable production or delivery effort, rights and compliance requirements, and economics where measurable. Define meaningful thresholds in the later research/validation phase; no thresholds are established here.

## Current status

No market study has been conducted in this phase. No niche has been selected and neither market is represented as validated.
