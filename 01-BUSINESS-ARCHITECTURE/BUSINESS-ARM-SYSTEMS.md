# Business Arm — AI Influencer Systems

## Purpose

Develop reusable operating methods, workflows, production frameworks, templates, and intellectual property that can support AIE and may later be offered externally.

## Potential audience and value

Potential users include internal AIE operations or future external creators and businesses. Their needs and willingness to pay are unknown.

## Potential revenue hypotheses

Templates, education, licensing, or productized workflows are possibilities to validate, not current offers or revenue.

## Capabilities and dependencies

Process design, documentation, testing, version control, maintainability, support planning, and IP review. Dependencies include evidence from actual work and clear rights to source material.

## Validation and risks

Test whether a method is repeatable, understandable, useful, differentiated, and maintainable before packaging it. Risks include premature productization, unsupported performance claims, accidental disclosure of client material, and IP ambiguity.

## Current boundary

AIE-OS is an organizational framework in this phase, not a software application or automation platform.
