# Quality and Compliance Standard

## Quality

Make deliverables accurate, readable, internally consistent, traceable to inputs, and appropriate to the approved scope. Check factual claims, names, identifiers, rights notes, and approvals before external use. Report unverified findings explicitly.

## Rights and responsible content

Track sources, licenses, permissions, and provenance for future media. Review likeness, voice, copyright, disclosure, privacy, and platform requirements before production or distribution. Obtain qualified guidance when the requirement is material or unclear; this document is not legal advice.

## Security and data

Use least necessary access, protect credentials outside tracked files, and minimize personal or client data. Do not place secrets in documents, examples, commits, or reports. Escalate suspected exposure promptly to the founder.

## Approval

Founder approval is required before purchases, external integrations, account access, contractual commitments, or publication. A quality check does not itself authorize release.
